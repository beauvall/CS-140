About David A. Bednar

David A. Bednar is a member of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles of The Church. He is known for teaching people to become stronger disciples of Jesus Christ through faith, prayer, and personal responsibility.

I like Elder Bednar because his messages are simple and practical. He often teaches that learning the gospel requires action and not just listening. His talks help me think about how I can improve and apply gospel principles in my everyday life.

"When you know who you are, then you know what to do." (David A. Bednar)
