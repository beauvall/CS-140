General Conference Information

Tamara W Runia

The Sculpting of Our Souls

Sister Runia taught that God understands everything we are going through. Even difficult and painful experiences can help shape us into the people God knows we can become."God is shaping us into something only He knows we can become."

Dale G. Renlund

“How Long Will It Be Ere They Believe Me?”

Elder Renlund taught that remembering God's goodness and trusting His promises can bring us peace and joy even when we are experiencing difficult situations. He also taught that we can find strength and hope through our faith in Jesus Christ and His Atonement.

Henry B. Eyring

“Fear Not, I Am with Thee”

President Eyring taught that challenges are part of mortal life, but Heavenly Father does not leave us alone. The Savior understands our grief and can strengthen us, sometimes through the kindness and service of other people."The Savior not only understands and has felt grief, but He has borne your griefs and carried your sorrows."
